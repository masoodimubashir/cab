Bookings and fares

Available routes, seats, vehicle types and service areas are shown in the app. Private fares may involve an offer and acceptance; fixed-route and shuttle bookings use the displayed route/seat price. Availability and estimated arrival times can change. Review the booking details and total before confirming or paying.

04

Cancellation, no-show and refunds

There is no universal three-minute free-cancellation window or flat cancellation fee across DreamCabs products. Review the cancellation information shown for your ride. Private cancellation may become unavailable when the driver is close to pickup or the ride has progressed beyond the cancellable stage.

Private and shuttle rides: where a customer cancellation is permitted before driver arrival, the cancellation charge is the configured percentage of the full fare, limited to the amount already paid online. The remaining online payment is refundable. After arrival or a customer no-show, the online payment may be forfeited. The online payment means the full fare for an online booking or the deposit for a cash booking.

Fixed-route seats: a permitted cancellation before the vehicle reaches your pickup is eligible for a refund of the online payment. After pickup arrival, boarding or a customer no-show, that payment is not refundable under the normal cancellation rules. Waiting/no-show windows depend on the route and city configuration.

When cancellation is caused by driver failure, the operator or the system, the applicable refund process returns the collected online payment for the affected booking. Refund requests can remain pending or require support if the payment provider fails. Any cash already paid to a driver requires separate resolution; the gateway cannot refund cash it did not collect.

Refunds are returned through the original payment process where supported. Bank/provider posting times vary; an approved or requested refund is not the same as money received. Contact dreamcabs2025@gmail.com with the booking ID for failed refunds, duplicate charges or service disputes. No instant wallet credit or fixed bank settlement time is guaranteed. These rules do not limit statutory remedies.

SOS alerts DreamCabs administrators with available trip/location information. It does not automatically contact police or your saved emergency contacts, and delivery or response is not guaranteed. Contact local emergency services directly when urgent assistance is needed. Do not rely on the app as an emergency-response service.

Trip sharing depends on network and location availability. Share tracking links only with trusted people. We do not promise continuous monitoring of every ride or automatic enforcement of driver duty/rest periods.

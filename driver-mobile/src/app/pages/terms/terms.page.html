Contractual Relationship & Technology Intermediary

02

Driver Eligibility & Mandatory KYC

Document Verification & Commercial Vehicle Standards

To register and remain active as an approved Driver-Partner on the DreamCabs Platform, you represent and warrant that you satisfy the following mandatory criteria:

  • Age Requirement: You are at least 18 years of age and hold legal contracting capacity under the Indian Contract Act, 1872.
  • Commercial Driving Licence (DL): You possess a valid, unexpired Commercial Driving Licence with appropriate transport vehicle endorsement issued by a competent Indian RTO.
  • Vehicle Registration (RC): The vehicle used on the Platform is registered as a commercial passenger transport vehicle (yellow plate) with an active Certificate of Registration (RC).
  • Fitness & Roadworthiness: An authentic, valid Vehicle Fitness Certificate issued by the regional transport authority.
  • Commercial Insurance: A comprehensive, active commercial passenger vehicle insurance policy covering third-party liabilities and passenger personal accident coverage.
  • Pollution Under Control (PUC): A valid, active emission compliance certificate.
  • Identity & Background Verification: Legitimate Aadhaar / PAN documentation and clear background / police verification records.
Document Expiry Obligation: You must submit updated document renewals prior to the expiry date of your DL, Insurance, RC, or Fitness. Expired documents will trigger automatic duty lock until approved by operations.
03

Operating Standards & Route Discipline

Fixed-Route Shared Mobility & Taxi Stand Paradigms

3.1. Corridor Route Adherence: When operating in Fixed-Route Shared Mobility or Shuttle Pooling modes, you must strictly follow the designated corridor route and stage stops / taxi stands approved for your vehicle. Arbitrary deviation without valid traffic/emergency justification is strictly prohibited.

3.2. Vehicle Cleanliness & Maintenance: Vehicles must be kept in clean, hygienic, mechanically sound, and roadworthy condition. Clean seat upholstery, operational seatbelts, working air conditioning/heating (as applicable), and functional safety equipment are mandatory.

3.3. Zero Tolerance for Offline Cash Solicitation: Driver-Partners matched with passengers via the Platform are strictly prohibited from bypassing the app, soliciting offline unmetered cash fares, or negotiating off-platform rides. Violations result in immediate permanent account termination and wallet forfeiture.

04

Fares, Driver Wallet & Daily Settlements

Commission Structures, Subscriptions & Payout Timelines

4.1. Fare Determination: Ride fares are computed transparently via DreamCabs algorithmic pricing based on route distance, passenger seat count, corridor stage tariffs, and prevailing aggregator norms.

4.2. Platform Commission & Subscriptions: DreamCabs deducts an agreed platform convenience fee/percentage commission or flat per-trip technology fee from gross fares. Driver-Partners may opt into weekly/monthly commission-free subscription plans where available.

4.3. In-App Wallet Gate: When passengers pay in cash, platform commission dues are automatically deducted from your in-app Driver Wallet. Driver-Partners must maintain a positive wallet balance above the minimum city threshold to remain eligible to go Online.

4.4. Bank & UPI Settlements: Digital fares collected through online payment gateways (UPI, Cards, Net Banking) are automatically credited to your Driver Wallet and disbursed to your verified bank account/UPI ID according to daily/weekly settlement schedules.

05

Duty Hours & Fatigue Management

MoRTH Aggregator Guidelines & Driver Well-Being
12 Hours Maximum Continuous On-Duty Window
10 Hours Mandatory Rest Break Period

In strict compliance with the Ministry of Road Transport and Highways (MoRTH) Motor Vehicle Aggregator Guidelines, 2020:

  • Driver-Partners are restricted from remaining logged on to the Platform for more than 12 continuous hours in a 24-hour cycle.
  • Upon reaching the 12-hour limit, the Platform will automatically switch your status to Offline and enforce a mandatory 10-hour rest period before you can go Online again.
  • Never operate a vehicle when fatigued, sleep-deprived, or unwell. Driver safety and passenger lives are the highest platform priority.

  • Emergency SOS Button: Both Driver and Passenger apps include an instant SOS trigger that immediately transmits live vehicle coordinates, driver credentials, and passenger manifests to designated emergency contacts and the Police Control Room.

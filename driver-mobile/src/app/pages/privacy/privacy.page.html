02

Background Location & GPS Tracking

Continuous Geolocation on Duty (Google Play & Apple Disclosures)
Prominent Background Location Disclosure: The DreamCabs Driver App collects continuous location data in the foreground AND background (even when the app is closed, minimized, or your device screen is locked) while you are on-duty / marked "Online".

Why Background Location is Required:

  • Real-time Ride Dispatch: Matches you with passengers requesting rides near your current moving position along fixed corridors and taxi stands.
  • Live Passenger ETAs & Tracking: Displays your live moving vehicle location on the passenger’s map while you are en route to pick them up and during active rides.
  • Corridor Route Adherence: Verifies adherence to designated shared mobility stages and stop stops.
  • Safety SOS Monitoring: Transmits accurate live coordinates to emergency services and police when an SOS trigger occurs.
  • Accurate Fare Calculation: Calculates transparent distance, waiting times, and corridor stage tariffs.

Note: Background location collection ceases immediately the moment you switch your status to "Offline" or log out of the application.

03

Information We Collect

KYC, Vehicle, Financial & Telemetry Data
  • Driver Identity & Contact: Full legal name, mobile phone number, email address, profile photograph, and language preference.
  • Statutory Commercial KYC: Commercial Driving Licence (DL) number and scans, Vehicle Registration Certificate (RC), Vehicle Fitness Certificate, Commercial Insurance Policy, Pollution Under Control (PUC) certificate, and PAN / Aadhaar records.
  • Vehicle Information: Vehicle make, model, year, fuel type, seating capacity, plate number, and assigned taxi stand / route scope.
  • Trip & Telemetry Logs: Pickup and drop coordinates, corridor route traversed, timestamps, vehicle speed, waiting duration, passenger manifest status, and customer ratings.
  • Financial & Payout Data: In-app wallet balance, digital trip earnings, commission dues, bank account number / IFSC code, and UPI virtual payment address (VPA) for settlements.
  • Device & Diagnostics: Device model, OS version, unique device identifier (UUID), push notification token (FCM/APNs), network carrier, IP address, and crash reports.

05

Third-Party Sharing & Disclosures

Need-to-Know Operational Disclosures

Personal and telemetry data is shared exclusively with authorized entities under strict confidentiality agreements:

  • Matched Passengers: When a ride is active, passengers see your name, photo, verified rating, vehicle model, and registration number.
  • Emergency SOS Authorities: If the SOS button is triggered, live GPS tracking and vehicle details are shared with your designated contacts and local Police authorities.
  • Payment Processors: Razorpay / Cashfree / UPI partners process payout disbursements under PCI-DSS Level 1 security.
  • Transport Authorities & Law Enforcement: Data is disclosed only when legally mandated by court order, statutory transport audits, or official law enforcement investigation.
06

Security & Data Retention

TLS 1.3, AES-256 Encryption & 7-Year Statutory Storage

6.1. Encryption & Safeguards: All data transmitted between the Driver App and our servers is encrypted using modern TLS 1.3 / SSL protocols. Sensitive KYC and financial records are encrypted at rest with AES-256.

6.2. Financial & Tax Retention: Trip transaction logs, tax invoices, and earnings records are retained for a statutory minimum of 7 years in compliance with Indian GST, Income Tax, and Motor Vehicle Aggregator regulations.

6.3. Account Deletion Purge: When an account deletion is requested, all personal identifiers are permanently deleted or anonymized within 30 days.

07

Your Legal Rights (India DPDP Act 2023)

Data Principal Rights & Autonomy

As a Data Principal under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you have the following enforceable rights:

  • Right to Access: Request a summary of all personal data held and processed by DreamCabs.
  • Right to Correction: Rectify inaccurate, outdated, or incomplete profile/KYC information directly via the app or support.
  • Right to Erasure / Deletion: Permanently delete your driver account and associated data via the in-app deletion button.
  • Right to Grievance Redressal: Submit data concerns to our Grievance Officer and escalate to the Data Protection Board of India if unresolved.
  • Right to Nominate: Nominate an authorized representative to exercise data rights in the event of death or incapacity.
08

In-App Account & Data Deletion

Apple App Store Guideline 5.1.1 & Google Play Data Safety Compliant

You have the absolute right to delete your driver account and purge your personal data at any time:

  • In-App Deletion: Navigate to Settings > Profile > Delete Account and confirm the deletion request via OTP.
  • Email Request: Send an email from your registered email address to dreamcabs2025@gmail.com with subject "Driver Account Deletion Request".

Upon confirmation, your active sessions are terminated immediately and your personal data is queued for complete erasure within 30 days.

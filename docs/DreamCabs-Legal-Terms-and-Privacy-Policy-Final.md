# 5. CANCELLATION, WAITING TIME, NO-SHOW & REFUND POLICY

### 1. Passenger Cancellation Policy
1.1. **Free Cancellation Grace Period**: Passengers may cancel any booked ride free of charge within **3 minutes** of booking confirmation, provided the Driver has not arrived at the pickup location.  
1.2. **Cancellation Fee**: If a Passenger cancels after 3 minutes of confirmation, or cancels after the Driver has already arrived at the designated pickup stop/stand, a standard cancellation fee (₹30 to ₹50 depending on route/mode) will be charged to cover driver dispatch fuel costs.  
1.3. **Scheduled Ride Cancellation**: Scheduled trips canceled less than 1 hour prior to pickup may attract a late cancellation charge.

### 2. Driver Cancellation Policy
2.1. Driver-Partners are expected to fulfill all accepted bookings. Frequent unprompted cancellations after accepting bookings negatively impact platform reliability and will lead to driver rating downgrades and temporary account throttling.  
2.2. A Driver may cancel without penalty if the Passenger fails to arrive within the mandatory waiting window or engages in abusive/unlawful conduct.

### 3. Waiting Time & No-Show Policy
3.1. **Standard Grace Period**: Upon reaching the designated pickup point/stand, the Driver will wait up to **5 minutes** free of charge.  
3.2. **Waiting Charges**: Beyond the 5-minute grace period, waiting charges (₹1.5 to ₹2 per minute) may accrue for private cab bookings where the passenger requested additional delay.  
3.3. **No-Show Threshold**: In **Fixed-Route / Shuttle Shared Pooling**, the driver cannot delay other seated passengers and will wait a maximum of **3 to 5 minutes**. If the passenger does not arrive within this window, the booking is marked as a **Passenger No-Show**, the vehicle departs, and the no-show fee is applied.

### 4. Refund Eligibility & Rules
Refunds are evaluated and granted under the following circumstances:
* **Driver Failure to Arrive**: If a driver accepts a ride but fails to arrive or cancels arbitrarily, any pre-paid digital fare is refunded 100% immediately.
* **Technical Error or Double Debit**: If an amount is deducted twice due to payment gateway network latency, the duplicate deduction is automatically refunded.
* **Service Disruption**: If a trip cannot be completed due to vehicle mechanical breakdown or force majeure, the pro-rata uncompleted fare will be refunded.
* **Fares for Completed Rides**: Fares for successfully completed trips are generally non-refundable unless an overcharging billing defect is verified.

### 5. Refund Processing Timelines & Modes
* **In-App Wallet**: Instant credit (within 1 to 2 hours) upon approval.
* **UPI & Net Banking**: 2 to 4 business days.
* **Credit / Debit Cards**: 5 to 7 business days, depending on the issuing bank’s settlement cycle.
* All refund inquiries may be submitted via `Settings > Help & Support > Request Refund` or by emailing `dreamcabs2025@gmail.com` with the Trip ID.

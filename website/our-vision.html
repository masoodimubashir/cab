Our Vision

A Message from
the Founder

Value people's time. That single idea is the heart of everything we build at DreamCabs.

UZ
Uzair Hameed Zargar Founder & Chairman DreamCabs KMR Pvt. Ltd.

At DreamCabs, we believe that transportation should adapt to people—not the other way around.

For years, I have watched students, office-goers, patients, senior citizens, and working professionals spend valuable time traveling to taxi stands, waiting for vehicles to fill up, or missing opportunities because transportation wasn't convenient. This daily struggle inspired the creation of DreamCabs.

Our vision is simple: value people's time.

With DreamCabs, passengers no longer need to leave their homes or workplaces just to secure a seat in a shared taxi. Instead, they can book their ride through the DreamCabs platform, choose a pickup point along a predefined route, and be picked up when the cab reaches that location.

The difference in practice

How DreamCabs changes travel

Consider the Kupwara–Srinagar route.

Today

A passenger from Drugmulla, Bremri, or any other village along this corridor often has to travel first to the Kupwara Sumo Stand simply to find a shared cab to Srinagar. This means extra travel, additional expense, and unnecessary waiting.

With DreamCabs

The passenger opens the DreamCabs app, selects the Kupwara–Srinagar route, chooses the nearest available pickup point—such as Drugmulla, Bremri, or another designated stop—and books a seat. The driver follows the fixed route and picks up the passenger at the selected location, eliminating the need to travel to the taxi stand first.

This makes travel:

More convenientMore affordableMore predictable SaferMore efficient

Most importantly, it gives people back something that cannot be replaced — their time.

The mission

More than a ride-booking platform

DreamCabs is a mission to make transportation smarter, more accessible, and more inclusive for every community, from rural villages to growing cities. By leveraging technology and fixed-route shared mobility, we aim to reduce unnecessary travel, increase vehicle occupancy, support local drivers with more consistent demand, and contribute to a cleaner, more sustainable future.

This is only the beginning. Our goal is to build a transportation network that connects people seamlessly while creating opportunities for drivers, strengthening local economies, and improving everyday life across India.

Thank you for believing in our vision and joining us on this journey.

Dream. Ride. Go.

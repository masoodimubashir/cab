Data Principal Protection & Data Localization Notice

DreamCabs complies strictly with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act), the Information Technology Act, 2000, and CERT-In Cyber Security Directions. All personal data is encrypted and stored exclusively on secure cloud servers located within the territory of India.

1. Introduction & Zero Data Selling Commitment

1.1. DreamCabs KMR Private Limited ("DreamCabs", "we", "us", "our") values your trust and is deeply committed to protecting the privacy, confidentiality, and autonomy of our Passengers and Driver-Partners.

1.2. This Privacy Policy governs all personal data collected through the DreamCabs Customer Mobile App, DreamCabs Driver App, website, and related technology APIs.

1.3. Zero Data Selling Guarantee: We do not sell, rent, monetize, or trade your personal data, travel history, or location telemetry to third-party data brokers, marketers, or advertisers under any circumstances.

2. Location Data & Real-Time Tracking

Location information is essential to power shared mobility, calculate accurate stage tariffs, and provide emergency SOS safety.

Passenger Foreground Geolocation

The Customer App collects location data only while the app is actively in use (foreground) to detect your nearest stop stage or taxi stand, render live moving cabs, and display real-time driver arrival countdowns.

Driver Background Geolocation (Prominent Disclosure)

Prominent Background Location Notice (Driver App):

The DreamCabs Driver App collects continuous location data in the foreground AND background (even when the app is closed or the device screen is locked) while the driver is on-duty and marked "Online".

Background tracking is required strictly for:

  • Real-time automated ride dispatch along fixed corridors and stands.
  • Transmitting live vehicle tracking and accurate arrival countdowns to waiting passengers.
  • Corridor route adherence verification and stage tariff computation.
  • Emergency SOS safety tracking with local police dispatch.

Background tracking terminates immediately the moment the driver toggles to "Offline" or logs out.

3. AI Voice Assistant & Audio Processing

3.1. When passengers use the AI Voice Assistant to search routes, query stop stages, or book seats, voice audio is streamed securely for instantaneous speech-to-text processing.

3.2. 24-Hour Automatic Deletion: Raw voice audio files are automatically and permanently deleted from server memory within 24 hours of query fulfillment.

3.3. Transcriptions are used exclusively to process your requested booking query and are never utilized for biometric profiling or commercial advertising.

4. Information We Collect

  • Identity & Profile: Full name, verified mobile phone number, optional email address, profile photo, and language preference.
  • Driver Statutory KYC: Commercial Driving Licence (DL), Vehicle Registration Certificate (RC), Vehicle Fitness, Commercial Insurance Policy, PUC Certificate, and PAN/Aadhaar details.
  • Trip & Telemetry Logs: Pickup/drop stop stages, GPS route traversed, timestamps, distance, speed, vehicle category, seat occupancy, and ratings.
  • Financial & Invoicing Data: Payment gateway transaction tokens, GST tax invoices, wallet balances, refund records, and driver bank account/UPI IDs for payouts. (Full card details and CVVs are never stored on our servers).
  • Device & Diagnostics: Device model, OS version, UUID, push notification tokens (FCM/APNs), network carrier, and crash logs.

6. Third-Party Sharing & Safeguards

Personal and telemetry data is shared strictly on a need-to-know basis:

  • Matched Passengers & Drivers: During an active booking, riders and drivers see essential trip coordinates, first names, and vehicle registration numbers.
  • Emergency SOS Authorities: Upon SOS activation, real-time GPS telemetry and vehicle details are shared directly with local Police Control Rooms and saved contacts.
  • PCI-DSS Payment Gateways: Licensed payment partners (Razorpay, Cashfree, UPI networks) process payments under PCI-DSS Level 1 compliance.
  • Statutory Compliance: Data is disclosed only when legally mandated by court order or statutory transport regulatory authorities.

7. Data Security & Retention Timelines

7.1. Encryption: All data transmissions are encrypted using TLS 1.3. Sensitive KYC documents, authentication secrets, and financial logs are encrypted at rest with AES-256.

7.2. Financial & GST Retention: In accordance with Indian GST, Income Tax, and Motor Vehicle Aggregator regulations, ride invoices and payment manifests are retained for a statutory minimum of 7 years.

7.3. Account Deletion Purge: When an account deletion is requested, all personal identifiers are permanently purged or anonymized within 30 days.

8. Your Legal Rights (India DPDP Act 2023)

As a Data Principal under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you have the following statutory rights:

  • Right to Access: Request a summary of all personal data held and processed by DreamCabs.
  • Right to Correction: Update inaccurate, outdated, or incomplete profile and KYC records.
  • Right to Erasure / Deletion: Permanently erase your account and personal data.
  • Right to Grievance Redressal: Direct unresolved data privacy concerns to our Grievance Officer and escalate to the Data Protection Board of India if unresolved.
  • Right to Nominate: Nominate an authorized representative to exercise data rights in case of death or incapacity.

9. In-App Account & Data Deletion (Apple & Google Compliant)

Users have the unconditional right to delete their account and personal data at any time:

  • In-App Self Service: Go to Settings > Profile > Delete Account and confirm with your mobile OTP.
  • Email Request: Send an email from your registered address to dreamcabs2025@gmail.com with subject "Account Deletion Request".

Upon confirmation, your active sessions are revoked immediately and personal data is queued for complete permanent purge within 30 days.

10. Children's Privacy

Our platform is not directed to individuals under 18 years of age without adult accompaniment. We do not knowingly collect personal information from minors. If you believe a minor has registered without consent, please contact us for immediate profile removal.

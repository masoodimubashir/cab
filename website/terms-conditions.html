This document is an electronic record published in accordance with the Information Technology Act, 2000 (Section 79), the Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules, 2020, and the Motor Vehicle Aggregator Guidelines (MoRTH 2020).

1.1. These Terms & Conditions ("Terms", "Agreement") constitute a legally binding agreement between DreamCabs KMR Private Limited ("DreamCabs", "we", "us", "our") and every individual who accesses or uses our website, mobile applications, or mobility services ("User", "Passenger", "Driver", "you").

1.2. Digital Technology Intermediary Status: DreamCabs operates an AI-powered smart mobility platform connecting passengers with independent commercial transport operators ("Driver-Partners"). DreamCabs operates strictly as a digital intermediary under Section 79 of the Information Technology Act, 2000. DreamCabs does not provide transport services itself, is not a common carrier, and does not own the commercial transport vehicles operated by Driver-Partners.

1.3. Contract of Carriage: When a passenger books a ride and a Driver-Partner accepts the assignment, a direct contract for transportation services is created between the passenger and that independent driver.

2. User Eligibility & Account Security

  • Age Requirement: You must be at least 18 years of age and legally capable of entering into binding contracts under the Indian Contract Act, 1872. Minors may travel only when accompanied by an authorized adult User who has booked the ride.
  • Mobile OTP Authentication: Registration requires a valid mobile phone number verified via One-Time Password (OTP). You are responsible for maintaining account confidentiality and all bookings initiated through your authenticated account.
  • Accurate Details: You agree to provide true, accurate, and up-to-date personal, emergency contact, and billing details.

3. Passenger Code of Conduct & Safety Rules

To ensure a safe, dignified, and comfortable transit experience for everyone, passengers agree to:

  • Punctuality: Arrive at designated pickup points, corridor stop stages, or taxi stands on time.
  • Mandatory Seatbelts: Wear seatbelts at all times during the trip as mandated by the Motor Vehicles Act, 1988.
  • Zero Tolerance for Harassment: Treat Driver-Partners and co-passengers with respect. Physical aggression, verbal abuse, discriminatory remarks, or sexual harassment results in immediate account deactivation and police reporting.
  • Prohibited Substances: Smoking, vaping, and the consumption or transport of alcohol, narcotics, contraband, hazardous chemicals, or weapons inside the vehicle are strictly prohibited.

4. Driver-Partner Obligations & Standards

  • Statutory Verification: Drivers must hold a valid Commercial Driving Licence (DL), commercial vehicle registration (RC), vehicle fitness certificate, commercial passenger insurance, and valid PUC certificate.
  • Safe Driving & Route Adherence: Comply with all traffic laws, speed limits, and designated corridor route stop stages. Driving under the influence of alcohol or drugs is strictly prohibited.
  • Professionalism & Hygiene: Maintain vehicle cleanliness, roadworthiness, passenger comfort, and professional courtesy at all times.

5. Booking Modes & Shared Corridors

5.1. Fixed-Route Shared Mobility: Vehicles operate along predetermined corridor routes with designated stop stages. In shared mode, other passengers may board or disembark along the route.

5.2. Private Cab Booking: Provides exclusive point-to-point, non-shared cab transportation between your chosen pickup and drop coordinates.

5.3. AI Voice Booking Assistant: Passengers may search routes and book seats using voice speech-to-text. Voice audio recordings are processed instantaneously and permanently deleted from server memory within 24 hours.

6. Fares, Dynamic Pricing Caps, Tolls & Payments

6.1. Fare Transparency: Upfront fare estimates displayed prior to booking include base fare, per-kilometer/corridor rates, and applicable GST.

6.2. Dynamic Pricing Caps: Dynamic demand pricing (if applicable) is strictly capped within statutory limits defined under MoRTH Motor Vehicle Aggregator Guidelines.

6.3. Tolls & Parking: Statutory highway toll plaza taxes and municipal parking charges incurred during the trip are payable in addition to the ride fare.

6.4. Payment Methods: DreamCabs supports secure PCI-DSS Level 1 digital payments (UPI, Credit/Debit Cards, Net Banking, In-App Wallet) and cash payment to the driver upon ride completion.

8. Safety, Live GPS Tracking & Emergency SOS

8.1. Emergency SOS Button: Triggering the in-app SOS immediately broadcasts live vehicle coordinates, driver details, and current route telemetry to pre-configured emergency contacts and local Police dispatch authorities.

8.2. Real-time Trip Sharing: Passengers can share live GPS ride tracking links with friends or family via SMS/WhatsApp with a single tap.

Upon confirmation, your login session is revoked immediately and personal data is queued for complete permanent purge within 30 days.

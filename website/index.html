FAQ

Questions, answered

01What is DreamCabs?+

DreamCabs is India's first AI-powered fixed-route shared mobility platform, connecting passengers and drivers with intelligent ride matching, voice booking and real-time tracking.

02How do I book?+

Choose your route, select the nearest pickup stand, and our AI instantly matches you to the best shared ride — book in seconds by tap or voice.

03Can drivers register?+

Yes. Registration is quick and easy. Drivers get optimized shared trips, reduced idle time, higher earnings and a clear performance dashboard.

04Is it safe?+

Every driver is verified. We provide SOS, trusted-contact sharing, live GPS tracking and dedicated women-safety features on every trip.

05How are fares calculated?+

Fares are based on AI-optimized fixed routes and shared occupancy, keeping costs low and transparent — you often save nearly half compared to solo rides.

06Can women travel safely?+

Absolutely. Verified drivers, SOS, trusted-contact sharing and real-time tracking are built in to keep every journey secure, day or night.

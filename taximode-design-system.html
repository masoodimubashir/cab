Design principles
The rules every screen should follow
01 / 08
PRINCIPLE 01
Bold green, dark ink, generous white
Primary actions use brand green. Surfaces stay near-white. The dark "ink" tone (#0F1419) is reserved for icon tiles, primary buttons, and pickup pins. Never decorate with random colors.
PRINCIPLE 02
Rounded, not sharp
Cards use 22px radius. Icon tiles 14px. Pills are fully round. Right angles feel wrong here — they break the friendly, modern character of the brand.
PRINCIPLE 03
Dark icon tiles on white
Every navigation, action, and category icon sits in a dark rounded square with a white glyph. This is the most recognizable pattern from the mobile app — preserve it in admin.
PRINCIPLE 04
Black pickup, green drop
Every route visualization uses a black pin for pickup and green pin for drop-off, connected by a green line. This pairing is sacred — never swap or recolor.
PRINCIPLE 05
Minimal formatting, maximal clarity
Use bold weights (700, 800) for hierarchy instead of color. Let the green do the work as an accent — over-using it weakens its meaning.
PRINCIPLE 06
Map-blue canvas, not pure grey
The page background (#F4F6FA) is a cool grey-blue that echoes the mobile app's map tile. Pure neutral grey feels sterile and off-brand.

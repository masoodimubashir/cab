Passenger Code of Conduct & Safety

Mutual Respect, Vehicle Hygiene & Mandatory Seatbelts

To ensure a safe, comfortable, and reliable transit experience for all passengers and drivers, you agree to:

  • Punctuality: Arrive at the designated pickup point, stop stage, or taxi stand on time before the vehicle departs.
  • Seatbelt Compliance: Wear seatbelts at all times during the trip as mandated by the Motor Vehicles Act, 1988.
  • Zero Tolerance for Harassment: Treat Driver-Partners and fellow co-passengers with respect. Verbal abuse, threats, discriminatory remarks, hate speech, or physical aggression result in immediate account ban and law enforcement referral.
  • Prohibited Items: Do not carry hazardous substances, inflammable materials, weapons, contraband, narcotics, or unpackaged offensive goods.
  • Smoking & Alcohol Ban: Smoking, vaping, and consumption of alcohol or illicit substances inside the vehicle are strictly prohibited.

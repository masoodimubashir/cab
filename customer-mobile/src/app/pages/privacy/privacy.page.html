01

Introduction & Privacy Commitment

Transparency, Accountability & Zero Data Selling

1.1. DreamCabs KMR Private Limited ("DreamCabs", "we", "us", "our") is committed to safeguarding the privacy and personal data of passengers using our mobility platforms ("Passenger", "Rider", "you", "your").

1.2. This Privacy Policy outlines our practices regarding data collection, storage, processing, and protection across the DreamCabs Customer Mobile Application (Android & iOS) and web services.

1.3. Zero Data Selling Commitment: DreamCabs does not sell, rent, monetize, or trade your personal information, travel patterns, or location logs to third-party advertisers or data brokers.

02

Location Data & Real-Time Tracking

Accurate Pickup, Live ETA & Emergency Dispatch
Foreground Location Notice: The DreamCabs Customer App collects precise location data while the app is in use to detect nearby pickup stops, display available shared cabs, and provide real-time turn-by-turn ride tracking.

Purposes of Location Processing:

  • Pickup & Drop Stage Selection: Automatically pinpoints your nearest corridor stop stage or taxi stand for faster booking.
  • Live Driver Approaching View: Shows real-time vehicle movement and precise arrival countdown on the interactive map.
  • Transparent Distance & Fare Calculation: Verifies actual route mileage and corridor stop tariffs for transparent upfront billing.
  • Emergency SOS Coordination: Immediately relays your real-time GPS coordinates to police authorities and emergency contacts upon SOS activation.
03

Voice Booking Assistant & Audio Processing

Instant Speech-to-Text & Automatic Audio Deletion

3.1. When you use the AI Voice Booking Assistant to search routes, query stop stages, or reserve cab seats, voice audio is streamed securely for instantaneous speech-to-text transcription.

3.2. Automated Audio Deletion: Raw audio recordings are automatically and permanently purged from server memory within 24 hours of query completion.

3.3. Transcribed text is utilized strictly to formulate your ride search or booking and is never used for behavioral advertising or biometric profiling.

04

Information We Collect

Profile, Trip History, Payment & Device Data
  • Identity & Account Details: Full name, mobile phone number, email address (optional), profile avatar, and preferred language.
  • Trip & Corridor Records: Pickup points, destination stop stages, booking timestamps, vehicle category, seat reservations, and driver ratings.
  • Financial & Billing Data: Payment method token, digital transaction ID, GST invoice history, wallet balance, and refund records. (Card CVV and bank passwords are never stored on our servers).
  • Emergency SOS Contacts: Contact names and phone numbers saved by you in your emergency safety circle.
  • Device & Telemetry: Device model, operating system version, unique device identifier (UUID), push notification token (FCM/APNs), network carrier, and crash analytics.

06

Third-Party Sharing & Safeguards

Authorized Service Providers & Lawful Disclosures

Your personal data is shared only under necessary operational and security parameters:

  • Assigned Driver-Partners: Drivers receive your first name, pickup location, drop stage, and masked phone number to complete the booked ride.
  • Emergency Services & Police: When SOS is triggered, live GPS telemetry and passenger details are transmitted to emergency responders and designated contacts.
  • Payment Processors: Licensed PCI-DSS Level 1 payment gateways (Razorpay, Cashfree, UPI networks) process payments without sharing card numbers.
  • Statutory Authorities: Data is disclosed only when strictly mandated under Indian law, court warrant, or official law enforcement inquiry.
07

Security & Data Retention

TLS 1.3, AES-256 Encryption & Statutory GST Retention

7.1. Enterprise-Grade Security: All data transmissions are encrypted using TLS 1.3. User profiles, authentication tokens, and trip histories are encrypted at rest with AES-256.

7.2. Financial & Tax Records: Tax invoices, payment logs, and ride manifests are retained for 7 years in compliance with Indian GST and transport aggregator regulations.

7.3. Account Deletion Purge: When you delete your account, personal identifiers are permanently purged or anonymized within 30 days.

08

Your Legal Rights (India DPDP Act 2023)

Data Principal Rights & Autonomy

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you enjoy full data autonomy:

  • Right to Access: Review all personal information and ride history associated with your registered profile.
  • Right to Correction: Update outdated profile details, emergency contacts, or email addresses instantly.
  • Right to Erasure / Deletion: Permanently erase your account and associated personal data at any time.
  • Right to Grievance Redressal: Direct unresolved data privacy concerns to our designated Grievance Officer.
  • Right to Nominate: Designate a representative to manage your data rights in the event of unforeseen incapacity.
09

In-App Account & Data Deletion

Apple App Store Guideline 5.1.1 & Google Play Data Safety Compliant

You have the right to permanently delete your passenger account and all associated personal data directly:

  • In-App Self Service: Go to Settings > Profile > Delete Account and confirm with your mobile OTP.
  • Email Request: Send an email from your registered address to dreamcabs2025@gmail.com with subject "Passenger Account Deletion Request".

Upon confirmation, your login session is revoked immediately and personal data is queued for complete permanent purge within 30 days.
